## 1. Summary

1. **One hot key holds most of the platform's power.** The owner's everyday wallet `13qRam63xqqd8KNUoAmHWQu7ro71oHqEsYHYaG5MsRiN` is, at slot 460705408: the legacy launchpad admin, the rewards registry admin, the platform fee recipient compiled into the live launchpad binary, the Meteora DBC config's fee claimer and leftover receiver, the authority of the site's address lookup table, the site's owner admin (`ADMIN_WALLETS`), and **one of the three members of the Squads multisig** whose vault is the upgrade authority of both live programs. See section 2.2.
2. **The Squads time lock is 0 seconds.** Any two of the three members can upgrade both live programs, or change the multisig itself, at once. v5 requires a 48-hour upgrade delay (master L2052).
3. **Legacy launches are open on chain** (`launches_paused = 0`, the one allowed config is enabled). v5 requires the legacy new-launch path to be disabled before new launches (master L2099-2100).
4. **These v5 roles do not exist yet:** dedicated treasury, stop-only guardian, founder selection publisher and verifier, hourly verifier pair, keeper, and an owner application admin key separate from the everyday wallet.
5. **The website holds no Solana signing key** today, which already matches v5 (section 2.1, row C16).
6. **No city coin exists on mainnet** (GET 3), so no founder, city reward config or coin reserve is exposed to any of the above today.
7. **The new main token's authorities are unknown.** Under owner amendment 01 the main token is a new mint that the owner has not supplied yet. Who can mint it and who can freeze it cannot be read until it is supplied, and both must be recorded at verification (C22, T11). Whoever holds a live mint authority over the main token could create balance and so decide founder selections and national ranks; that is why the authorities are recorded and disclosed before any selection or ranking reads the mint.

## 5. Guardian and runbook notes

These are operating rules for the target; none has been rehearsed on mainnet.

| situation | who acts | how | what it cannot do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stop (incident) | T8: any member proposes, a second approves and executes | set the halt flags to true; cancel a pending selection or a pending hourly root | resume, replace a founder, upgrade, withdraw. The draft stop tool was built for a 1-of-3 guardian (one transaction creates, approves and executes, `curve:VICINITY-CURVE-DESIGN.md:3909`); under v5's 2-of-3 it must be changed to a two-member flow, which is a slower brake by the master's choice |
