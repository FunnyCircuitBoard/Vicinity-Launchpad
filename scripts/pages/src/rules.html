City founders

Earned over time, chosen by locals
  1. Qualify: hold 100K–1M by city size $VICINITY in every balance check for 7 days. Checks happen at random times, about once an hour, so borrowing tokens for a few minutes doesn't work.
  2. Be local: your home community is set by a location check at least 7 days before you apply.
  3. Apply standing inside the city. The first qualified claimer becomes Seed Steward at once — no election of one (see below).
  4. Challenge: if a verified local disagrees, 10 endorsements from verified locals in 72 hours force an election the steward runs in.
  5. Score when a window closes (published with its hash):

score = 50% × endorsement share + 30% × contribution + 20% × holdings holdings count from the amount up to 2× the amount (more counts the same) ties: lowest sha256("vicinity-seat|window|wallet")

  1. Objections: 48 hours for locals to object; an admin who didn't object decides. Upheld → the next applicant.
  2. Grace: below the amount (in a check, or before any sensitive action) → moderation pauses at once, 7 days to fix it (48 hours for a Seed Steward). More than 2 graces in 90 days → released.
  3. Released: the city reopens; the former founder waits 30 days before applying anywhere.
  4. The city's coin: the active founder designs it: its name, a short pitch, a colour, a logo, and what it's paired with on Raydium LaunchLab (SOL, USDC or RAY). The ticker is the city's own, so nobody can copy it. Every design is public and every change is in the log. Once it launches, the founder sends its contract, someone else (an admin) checks and records it, and the design is locked for good.
The Stake Ladder

Big city, bigger stake

The founder's holding bar is set by city size — a hamlet shouldn't demand a metropolis-sized stake, and a metropolis shouldn't be claimable with pocket change. Your bar is fixed when you claim; founders already seated keep theirs, and bars are re-tiered quarterly.

stake = 100,000 × (population ÷ 10,000)^(1/3), clamped 100,000–1,000,000, rounded down to 10,000 rule of eight: 8× the people → 2× the stake

Seed Steward

Claim it, prove it, keep it
  • Immediate: the first qualified claimer of an unclaimed city becomes Seed Steward on the spot and can mint the city's coin as a trial founder.
  • Probation: 90 days bonded. Confirmed automatically at 90 days of good behavior, or earlier once 50 verified local holders back the city.
  • Challenged: 10 endorsements from verified locals during probation force an election — which the steward runs in and defends at the same bar.
  • Resign: walk away any time; the bond is released and the city reopens.
  • Dark cities: a city with no founder for 30 days can be adopted by any holder — but a verified local challenger keeps priority.
Squad Founding

Found it together
  • 3–5 verified locals can pool their holdings to meet the bar — the 7-day tenure applies to the pooled sum.
  • One wallet is designated the founder and mints the coin; every member is recorded as a co-founder.
  • The seat's balance is the squad's combined holdings: if the pool drops below the bar, the whole seat goes to grace.
Country managers

Elected for a term, not handed to the richest
  • Candidates: founders active for 30+ days, not banned, not just finishing a second term in a row.
  • Voters: verified locals of the country, one vote each.
  • Election: opens automatically, runs 7 days, result and hash published.
  • Term: 90 days, at most two in a row. Powers pause while the manager's seat is in grace.

score = 50% × vote share + 30% × service + 20% × holdings (capped at 2×) service = time as founder (up to 180 days) + confirmed moderation − overturned decisions

Who may vote or endorse: an account and a home community set at least 7 days before the vote opened, and at least one check-in there (being physically present). One vote per person.

Moderation

Nobody moderates alone
  • Hide: a founder (their city), a manager (their country) or an admin, always with a reason. It comes back after 24 hours unless a second moderator confirms it or 3 people reported it.
  • Reports: 5 reports hide a post until a moderator reviews it.
  • Bans: proposed by one person, approved by a different one; they last 30 days.
  • Appeals: once per decision, judged by someone who wasn't involved.
  • Public log: every action, with who (role and name), why and what happened. See the log ↗
Founding Supporters

Counted fairly, with no sign-up

eligible = the lower of (balance at the cutoff) and (average of the last 14 days) pools, bonding curves and team wallets are excluded

  • The cutoff is always 00:00 UTC and announced ahead: not scheduled yet.
  • Published with a Merkle root and a hash of every input, then 48 hours to challenge before it's final.
  • Check any wallet and get its proof on the Launchpad page.
Location & accounts

Private, and hard to fake
  • Your location is read in exactly one place. It returns a signed note naming your community (valid 5 minutes, usable once), and the coordinates are thrown away.
  • VPNs, proxies and far-away connections are refused, with one generic answer, so nobody can probe which check they tripped.
  • One account per wallet and per Google login or verified e-mail. It makes fake accounts harder, but no online check can prove someone is one unique person (one inbox can have several addresses): that's why votes also need age and a check-in.
  • What we keep: your wallet address; with Google only the account id and first name; with e-mail the address itself (it is your account id) and a hash of the code (10-minute life); a made-up username; your home community; and, only if you add them, a contact e-mail and a phone number you can remove (the phone number is not verified and not used yet). We never keep a password anyone could read (an e-mail account that has one keeps only a salted hash of it), and check-in coordinates are never stored.
  • Member profiles: other signed-in members can open your profile and see your username, home community, bio, wallet address, member-since date, badges and your $VICINITY and city-coin balances. They never see your real name, how you signed in, your e-mail or phone, or your location. You can block anyone, and there is no messaging.
  • Sensitive actions (applying, endorsing, voting, moderating) need your wallet proven in the last 30 minutes.
Never

What nobody can ever do
  • Move, freeze or take anyone's tokens.

Rules change only as a new, published version. Seats, elections and snapshots keep the version they were decided under.
