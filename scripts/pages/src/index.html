FAQ

Questions

What is Vicinity?

A map of every real city, where each community gets one official coin, local leaders and its own feed for memes, check-ins, discussions and weekly votes. $VICINITY is the token that gets you in first — it launches October 3 on Raydium LaunchLab. From October 10 the Vicinity Launchpad lets each city launch its coin.

How do I buy $VICINITY?

Get a Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare or Backpack), add SOL, then use the Buy on Raydium button on the Token page. It opens the official $VICINITY on Raydium LaunchLab (raydium.io). Look-alike sites that copy Raydium are scams that empty wallets.

Why is $VICINITY launching on Raydium LaunchLab and not on the Vicinity Launchpad?

Trust. LaunchLab is Raydium's own launchpad on Solana: no presale or private sale, and everyone buys on the same public curve. When the curve fills, the coin moves into a Raydium pool, minting is switched off for good, and the pool's liquidity is burned or locked permanently. Launching our own token on our own brand-new launchpad would mean asking you to trust code we wrote and control. We'd be the player and the referee.

Timing. The Launchpad opens on October 10 at 10:10:10 AM New York time, and $VICINITY is what gets people in first — so it launches a week earlier, on October 3, on Raydium LaunchLab.

Room to grow. LaunchLab lets projects run their own launchpads on the same Raydium infrastructure. Starting $VICINITY there keeps it on the rails the Vicinity Launchpad can build on.

Focus. The Launchpad is built for city coins: one official coin per city, verified locals, founders and managers. $VICINITY is the key to that map, not a city coin, so it stays where it launched.

How do I get my dashboard?

Connect a Solana wallet, sign one free message (it isn't a transaction), then sign in with Google or verify an e-mail address. Your rank and your city are checked automatically, and you're in. One account per wallet and per login.

I use FOMO (or another app wallet). How do I connect?

If the app can't connect to websites, choose "App wallet (FOMO…)" on the connect page. We'll show a tiny, exact amount of SOL to send to yourself. It costs only the network fee, and since only the wallet's owner can send from it, it proves the wallet is yours. You can also open this site inside a wallet app's browser, or scan a code with a wallet app on your phone.

What if I don't live inside any city?

Then you pick one of the three nearest communities to join. You can also ask for your town to become its own community; your Country Manager approves it.

What happens if I sell?

Badges that need a balance go on the next check. A City Founder who drops below their city's founder amount loses moderation powers at once and has 7 days to hold again (48 hours for a Seed Steward); after that (or after a third grace within 90 days) the seat reopens, and they wait 30 days before applying again.

How do I become a City Founder?

Hold enough $VICINITY for your city's size (100K–1M on the Stake Ladder) for 7 days in a row (balances are checked at random times), have your home set in the city for 7+ days, and apply from inside its boundary. The first qualified claimer becomes Seed Steward at once — a trial founder on a 90-day probation. If 10 verified locals disagree, they force an election you run in; otherwise you're confirmed at 90 days (or 50 local holders). A squad of 3–5 locals can also pool holdings to found together. Locals have 48 hours to object to any new seat. One person, one city.

Who is the Country Manager?

A City Founder elected by the country's verified locals for 90 days (50% votes, 30% service, 20% capped holdings), at most two terms in a row. Until a country has one, the admin covers it.

Can a whale take over?

No. Holdings only count up to 2× the founder amount, founders are chosen mostly by local endorsements, managers are elected, and every vote is one person, one vote. Balances are checked at random times, so renting tokens for a day doesn't work either. See every rule.

Is this a rug pull?

No. There's no presale, and when the LaunchLab curve fills, Raydium switches minting off for good and burns or locks the pool's liquidity. Every team wallet is listed on this site. The Token page shows, live from the blockchain, whether minting and freezing are switched off.

Which contract address is real?

Only the one on the Token page. Paste any address into the checker to confirm. Contract addresses can't be posted in Vicinity feeds, so a fake can't spread there.

What do you store about me?

Your wallet address. With Google sign-in, only your Google account id and first name. With e-mail sign-in, the e-mail address itself (it is your account id) and a hash of the 6-digit code, which expires in 10 minutes. A made-up username, which is the only name feeds show, never your wallet. The name of your home community. Optionally a contact e-mail and a phone number: you can remove them any time, and the phone number is not verified and not used for anything yet. Never your location: it's used once to find your community, then thrown away. No passwords. Member profiles: other signed-in members can open your profile and see your username, home community, bio, wallet address, member-since date, badges and your $VICINITY and city-coin balances. They never see your real name, how you signed in, your e-mail or phone, or your location. You can block anyone, and there is no messaging.

Can someone fake their location?

We make it hard: GPS must be inside the real boundary, and the internet connection must be in the same country and nearby. VPNs, proxies, cloud servers and Tor are refused. Country Managers can flag anything suspicious.

Is this financial advice?

No. Meme coins are very risky and can go to zero. Only use money you can afford to lose.
