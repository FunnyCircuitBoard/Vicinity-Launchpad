How to check a city coin yourself

A city coin is official only if its mint address is on the list above (or in official-coins.csv). For each coin, the table and the JSON show its coin record, its mint and its pool as this site read them from the chain. To check them yourself, ask any Solana RPC node for the coin record (getAccountInfo; coinAccount in the JSON): its owner is the launchpad program listed above; its data starts with the 8 bytes d7c335eed9c4d533, which mark a coin record, followed by the city id (8 bytes, little-endian); its address is the one derived from the launchpad program and the seeds "coin" and that city id, so each city has one; and in its data the mint starts at byte 48, the price-curve setting at byte 112 and the pool at byte 144 (32 bytes each). Then ask for the pool: its owner is Meteora's bonding-curve program, and in its data the creator (byte 104) is the coin record, the price-curve setting (byte 72) is the record's and one listed above, and the mint (byte 136) is the coin's. Our programs' description (IDL) is not published yet, so Solscan shows the record's data as raw bytes; the byte offsets above are how to read it. Do not go by a pool's creator alone: anyone can open a pool on our public price-curve setting and then hand its creator role to one of our records, and anyone can make an empty account of their own show our program as its owner. The list above starts from our coin records and makes all of these checks.
