  1. A local account

    Wallet + Google or e-mail, with this city set as home at least 7 days before you apply.

  2. Hold your city's founder amount for 7 days

    100,000 to 1,000,000 $VICINITY: small towns need less, big cities more. Balances are checked at random times about every hour, so borrowed tokens don't count. Holdings above 2× only count as 2×.

  3. Apply standing in the city

    Your location is checked once against the real boundary, with your internet connection. VPNs are blocked. We never save it.

  4. Locals decide

    The first qualified claimer becomes Seed Steward on the spot, with a 90-day probation locals can challenge. If several claim together, a 72-hour window scores 50% endorsements, 30% contribution, 20% holdings.
